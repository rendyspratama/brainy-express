How we teach

Brainy Express blends four ideas from classrooms around the world. Each one shapes how every game is built.

From Singapore

Touch, picture, symbol

New ideas start with things to drag and hold, then become pictures, then symbols. Your child moves up a level after two good rounds, so understanding comes before speed.

From Finland

Play first, keep it short

Sessions last 3 to 5 minutes. There are no timers by default and no rankings against other children. A calm balloon is one tap away.

From the United States

Wonder and grow

Pip asks open questions like “What do you think will happen?” and praises effort and strategy. A quick feelings check-in opens and closes each ride.

From China

Steady daily practice

A 2-minute warm-up mixes earlier levels for spaced review. Pip shows a worked example before every new challenge, step by step.
